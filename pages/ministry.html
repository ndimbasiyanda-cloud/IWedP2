PRAYER

Lord, Thank You for being my shepherd. You guide me, provide for me, and protect me. I confess that I sometimes doubt Your care. Help me trust You more. Teach me to rest in Your promises and rely on Your provision. Forgive me for seeking security in the wrong places. Give me a heart that is content in You alone. Strengthen my faith so that I walk in confidence, knowing that You are always with me. Thank You for Your love and faithfulness.
